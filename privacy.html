TORY Autodoklady — Privacy Policy

Development version 0.1.0 · 13 November 2026

This app stores vehicle names, registration plates, document dates, amounts, notes and photographs locally on your device. It does not require an account. This version contains no advertisements, analytics or app-operated backend.

Photographs and text recognition

Photos are selected by you or captured using the device camera. Android uses a bundled Google ML Kit text recognition model; iOS uses Apple Vision. Recognition is performed on the device. Suggested dates and amounts must be checked against the original document. The Android app removes Internet and network-state permissions from its merged manifest.

Notifications

With your permission, local notifications remind you about recorded expiry dates. Notifications use generic text rather than showing vehicle identifiers or document contents on the lock screen. The operating system controls delivery. The app cannot guarantee delivery and does not determine legal deadlines.

Export and backup

You can export a JSON backup containing your records and photographs, or share a PDF report. You choose the destination and recipient. If you choose a cloud provider or another app, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. Exported backups are not password-encrypted by this app; use a trusted, protected destination. Automatic app backups are disabled/excluded in this version. Deleting the app can remove its local data.

Deletion and control

You can delete individual documents or vehicles in the app. Deleting a vehicle also deletes its attached records. Exported copies must be deleted at their destination. Restore replaces the current local data only after confirmation.

Support

Contact: szollosi.e11@gmail.com. Do not send private documents unless necessary for your support request. If you email us, your email provider and ours handle that communication.

This policy describes the current development build. It must be reviewed when purchases, integrations or other data-handling features change.
